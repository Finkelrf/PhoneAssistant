3 · Voice: ask Claude out loud

Say the wake word (or tap the mic) and ask a question. Claude answers out loud, using the Remote server's own Claude sign-in (your plan).

a · Install Remote's certificate on the phone (once)

The phone only lets a page use the microphone over HTTPS. Remote makes its own certificate that only works for this computer's address.

  1. On the phone, open /ca.crt here: Download certificate
  2. Android asks for a name: type Remote, choose VPN and apps, and confirm. (Android may ask you to set a screen lock first.) If nothing opens, go to Settings › Security › Encryption & credentials › Install from storage and pick remote-ca.crt from Downloads.

b · Wake word

Pick the engine on the phone: Settings › Voice › Wake word.

openWakeWord (default, nothing to sign up for): the phone streams its microphone to this computer over the home network, and the computer listens for the word. Ready-made words: Hey Jarvis (default), Alexa, Hey Mycroft, Hey Rhasspy.

Your own word, such as “Hey Claude”: train it with openWakeWord's training notebook (search “openWakeWord automatic model training”), then upload the .onnx file here:

Picovoice Porcupine (optional): the word is heard on the phone itself. Needs a free AccessKey from console.picovoice.ai (it doesn't accept Gmail-type addresses).

Your own Porcupine word: train it in the Picovoice console for Web (WASM) and upload the .ppn file:
